# Qualified legal references

This page is informational project documentation, not legal advice. **Verify current wording, applicability, and official guidance before relying on any summary.**

- UIDAI publishes guidance on responsible use and sharing of Aadhaar information, including the distinction between masked and unmasked Aadhaar. See the official [UIDAI circulars and advisories](https://uidai.gov.in/en/about-uidai/legal-framework.html).
- The Aadhaar Act and associated regulations govern authentication, identity information, and security obligations in their applicable contexts. The project does not infer that every disclosure of a land record is automatically unlawful; the facts, authority, purpose, and applicable rules matter. See the official [Aadhaar Act resources](https://uidai.gov.in/en/about-uidai/legal-framework/aadhaar-act.html).
- Fingerprints and other biometrics are treated by this project as sensitive information requiring conservative handling. This is a project safety policy and should not be read as a universal legal conclusion. Consult current official UIDAI material and qualified counsel.

## Project policy versus law

The scanner masks Aadhaar/VID and redacts fingerprint regions by default, blocks uncertain or failed verification, keeps notices as human-review drafts, and restricts the demo to synthetic data. These are engineering controls and recommendations; they do not replace legal analysis or mandatory controls.
